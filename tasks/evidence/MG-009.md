## Contract clarification (approved by Shabaz): `missing_modality_rule`
`DetectorConfig` = exactly `schema_version, scenario_id, association_tolerance_s, threshold, method_name, missing_modality_rule`. Any other field, such as truth, counts or an ExperimentConfig, gives `mineguard:InvalidDetectorConfig`. This names the "explicit missing-modality rule" that MG-003 v1.0 already requires; no other field is added.

| rule | thermal_only / em_only | or | and | weighted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `abstain` | decides when its own channel is valid | needs both | needs both | needs both |
| `single_valid` | same as abstain | uses the present channel's ratio score | **still needs both** | uses the model's frozen `weighted_fallback` thresholds; refused if the fallback is absent |
| `require_both` | decides only on paired (both-valid) candidates | needs both | needs both | needs both |

## Method definitions (as implemented)
- **Features** use TRAIN statistics: zT = (T − bg_T)/scale_T and zE = (R − bg_E)/scale_E. The thermal score is |zT|, a signed-contrast magnitude, so cool anomalies count too. The EM score is zE, which is one-sided.
- **Decision scores:**
  - thermal_only: |zT|
  - em_only: zE
  - OR: max(|zT|/τT, zE/τE)
  - AND: min(|zT|/τT, zE/τE)
  - weighted: 0.5·|zT| + 0.5·zE
  - Here τT and τE are the validation-selected single-channel thresholds. The weights are pre-declared, not fitted.
- **Association:**
  - Each valid thermal sample (taken in t_s then sample_id order) gets the closest unused valid EM sample with |dt| ≤ `association_tolerance_s`.
  - Ties go to the earlier t_s, then the smaller sample_id.
  - No sample is used twice.
  - Invalid or NaN samples never enter a decision.
- **Clustering** is fixed and pre-declared: within a 1.0 s window and within 0.75 m of the running centroid. confidence_0_1 = NaN, because it is not calibrated. uncertainty_m = the declared `gps_sigma_m`, or NaN.
- **Scoring** follows MG-008 §5–6:
  - The unit is the evaluable survey cell, on the MG-005 grid.
  - The confusion counts are cell-level, and several events in one cell count once.
  - Unassessed cells are excluded and counted separately.
  - Undefined ratios are NaN.
  - Localization is separate: a maximum-cardinality, minimum-distance one-to-one `matchpairs` within 0.75 m.
- **Provenance:** `checkProvenance` rejects mismatches in scenario_id, schema, seeds, sensor or model version, or config checksum (`mineguard:ProvenanceMismatch`). It runs for every scored trial.

Known limitations:
- **SIMULATION ONLY.** The sensors are the MG-007 proxies with assumed parameters. These metrics describe this synthetic setup; they are not airborne mine-detection performance, not a clearance rate and not a safety statement.
- The test set is very small (4 groups, 6 positive cells). The results only show the pipeline working and the trade-offs between methods; they are not statistically meaningful performance estimates.
- Flat terrain only; one sensor fixture; fixed altitude for the nominal splits. Material assignment happens at the experiment level, using seeded post-generation material labels.
- The weights (0.5/0.5), clustering window and radius, and match radius are predeclared and were **not** selected on validation. This is a reviewer-approved exception to MG-008 §4.4, and these values are not optimised.

- confidence_0_1 is always NaN, because no calibration is fitted. uncertainty_m is the declared geotag sigma only, not an estimated localization uncertainty.
- Thresholds are fitted for nominal conditions. The stress results (noise and soil shift) show severe miscalibration under domain shift, and this baseline has no adaptive background.
- The optional second outputs (diagnostics, report) are reviewer-approved additive extensions. The frozen first outputs and the 4-input signatures are unchanged and covered by test x01.
- The 4-group TEST set has already been inspected. It is accepted for engineering verification only and must not be expanded or retuned; later held-out evaluation uses the MG-014 locked plan.
