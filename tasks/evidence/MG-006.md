## Verified design decisions

- **Thermal**: `temperature_K`; explicit model baseline, signed/zero contrast, soil/time/depth sensitivity, nadir footprint area and dilution, seeded noise; zero target and harmless hot rock controls; no claim to solve heat/moisture PDE without actually implementing it.
- **EM**: `response_norm` dimensionless; configurable monotone response shape without claiming coil calibration; **ground clearance plus burial depth** in geometry; nonmetal response null, metallic debris clutter and background/noise; explicit model cutoff yielding invalid/NaN, no guaranteed 5 m efficacy.
- **Frozen integration**: MG-003 `simulateThermal(config,scenario,poses)`, `simulateEM(config,scenario,poses)`, SensorSample exactly 11 fields, ENU metres, timestamps seconds, local per-channel RNG and private truth firewall. Neither changes MG-004 generator or MG-005 navigation.
- **No target-class information in observations**: the forward physics may read restricted truth; outputs must not contain truth ID, class label or true coordinates. Classification and thresholding are **MG-008/MG-009**, not MG-007.
