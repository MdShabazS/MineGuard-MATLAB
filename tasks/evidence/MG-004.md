## Design (as implemented)
- **Determinism:** a local `RandStream('mt19937ar','Seed',config.seed)`. The global RNG is not touched, and the test asserts that `rng` is unchanged. Channel seeds are derived as `mod(noise_seed + k*1000003, 2^32)` (k = 1 thermal, 2 EM, 3 navigation) and recorded in `environment.rng`.
- **Placement:** uniform rejection sampling within `[x_min,x_max]×[y_min,y_max]`. A minimum separation applies across all objects (targets and clutter), and optional exclusion polygons are avoided.
- **Bounded failure:** (1) a hexagonal-packing capacity upper bound gives an immediate error; (2) a budget of 2000 placement attempts per object; (3) a hard cap of 10,000 objects. Every failure raises `mineguard:InvalidScenario` with a message that names the cause.
- **Truth firewall:** `geometry` has exactly the 6 frozen fields: `schema_version, scenario_id, bounds_enu_m, terrain_id, terrain_model, exclusion_polygons_enu_m`. Truth is only in the separate `truth` table. Object IDs are neutral (`OBJ-0001`…), and targets and clutter are shuffled.
- **Environment:** soil and terrain IDs, plus thermal/EM parameters stored as `value=NaN`, with units and `status="PLACEHOLDER_NOT_CALIBRATED"`. No physical constants are claimed.

Known limitations:
- **SIMULATION ONLY.** Objects, depths and materials are synthetic placeholders. Nothing here is a real minefield, and no detection capability is claimed.
- Terrain is a flat datum only (S0 tier). Variable terrain height (S2) is not implemented.
- C-05: the navigation-interface part (signatures and truth independence) is verified in MG-005 by `testMG005Navigation/c05_navigationTruthFirewall`, not here.
- `exclusion_polygons_enu_m` in the config is an optional extension and not part of the frozen required `ExperimentConfig` fields. With no exclusions, the geometry field is an empty cell.
- Bitwise determinism was checked only on this R2026b installation and was not tested across releases or platforms.
- The near-capacity C-07 case uses random rejection sampling, so it may place all objects or stop at the attempt budget. Both outcomes are bounded and validated. It was not run on CI because GitHub CI does not run MATLAB.
