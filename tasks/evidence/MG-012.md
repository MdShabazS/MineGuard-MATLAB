## Scientific/technical checks (static only)

- **Frozen interface:** Public `mineguard.rl.selectSurveyCell(observation,allowedCells,policy)` signature and the existing MG-003 table/struct field names are not changed. MG-012 proposes a versioned adapter for implementation in MG-013.
- **Ground-truth firewall:** Neither policy observation nor reward accesses `TargetTruth`, target labels/depth/count/location, scenario `hidden_truth`, evaluator-only training/test truth, or future hidden objects. Safe known geometry and historical observed poses are allowed.
- **Shared comparison population:** PPO actions use the same real fixed-grid cell-index convention and legal exclusion rules as MG-005; coverage is defined by actual virtual pose samples, not waypoint intent. Baseline infeasibility must be labelled rather than an automatic PPO win.
- **Reward:** Only legal new coverage and non-secret route budget, repeated movement and invalid action penalties. No target-hit reward.
- **Held-out evaluation:** Policy hyperparameters/checkpoint chosen on training/validation only; same route budgets and scenario groups for PPO vs fixed grid; paired metrics, uncertainty and failure disclosure. Model results cannot claim physical minefield safety or detection accuracy.
- **No guaranteed superiority:** PPO may lose to conventional grid, especially on small rectangular synthetic fields. The task accepts valid negative results.
