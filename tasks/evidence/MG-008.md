## Design verification (static documentation review, not MATLAB test results)

- Function signatures match MG-003: `detectAnomalies(detectorConfig,poses,samples,model)` and `scoreDetections(config,events,truth,runMetadata)`.
- Public `DetectionEvent` preserved with its 12 exact fields, while `ExperimentResult` frozen field names remain unchanged.
- The specification rejects target truth in navigation, fusion and detector input. Only forward physics/evaluator may access `TargetTruth`.
- No fake performance number, arbitrary real coil range, or implied licensed Sensor Fusion and Tracking Toolbox.
- Event/object one-to-one matches have no intrinsic TN universe, so standard TN/FPR uses the declared **fixed-cell** assessment population; alert/localization matching has separate metrics and denominators.
- Scenario grouping prevents train/test leakage from repeated frames and derived channel noise seeds.
- Missing and out-of-model sensor readings stay invalid with NaN rather than becoming an artificial zero or assumed negative class.
