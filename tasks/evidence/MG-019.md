## Grid compatibility (Phase 2)
- The policy is fixed at **25 actions / 55-element observation**, which means the MG-013 5×5 environment: 10×10 m, 2 m cells, cell IDs, legal legs and coverage exactly as in MG-005.
- MG-014's 20×20 m families have **100 cells**. Calling `runSurveyMission(..., "ppo")` on such a config throws `mineguard:IncompatiblePolicy` before any inference, and `selectSurveyCell` also rejects a 100-cell table (t04).
- There is **no padding and no 100-cell PPO**. That would be a separate policy needing new training and its own protocol, and it is not part of MG-019.

## Engineering analysis (Phase 8)
- **Did PPO outperform the grid?** No. It lost all 8 comparable scenarios in the fresh set and all 4 in the replication.
- **Coverage:** worse, by 0.04–0.22 per scenario.
- **Distance and time:** more of both (+3.2 m and +1.6 s on average). It spends the 60 m budget on long diagonal legs and revisits, then stops at `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, typically one or more cells short.
- **Revisits:** more than the grid (2.2 against about 0.1).
- **Invalid actions:** yes. 2 of the 20 fresh missions failed by repeating rejected actions (43 and 48); 7315 covered only 0.409 of the legal cells. That is a robustness defect of the deterministic policy.
- **Robustness:** moderate where there are no or few no-fly zones (0.96 coverage), weaker with interior zones (0.68–0.96), and one severe failure.
- **Sensor observations:** the routes visibly change where readings are taken (screenshots 02 and 03), but at 2 m the detector is at chance level, so routing has no supported effect on detection.
- **PPO's one practical advantage:** it can produce a legal route where the MG-005 grid refuses (interior no-fly zones), reaching 0.68–0.96 coverage. That reflects a limitation of the baseline (no rerouting), not an optimal PPO. A rerouting grid planner would be the fair comparison there.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Keep the **fixed grid as the release default**, as MineGuard already does.
  - Retain PPO as **experimental / optional**: integrated, demonstrable and honestly labelled.
  - Any improvement belongs in a separate future task: action masking or a learned validity penalty, more training, a rerouting grid baseline, and a 100-cell policy with its own preregistered protocol.
