## Model summary (as implemented; see the function headers)
**Thermal** is a phenomenological proxy, NOT a heat-equation solver:
- `T = T_bg + Σ A·g_i·exp(−depth_i/L)·phase(t)·w_i + ε`
- `A` is the signed contrast amplitude, and A = 0 is the negative control.
- `g_i` is a signed per-material gain. A hot rock (3.0) can exceed a target (1.0).
- `phase` is 1 in `static` mode. In `cosine` mode it is `cos(2π(time_of_day_s + t_s − contrast_peak_time_s)/86400)`.
- `w_i` is the fraction of the nadir footprint disc (radius `standoff·tan(half-FOV)`) covered by an object disc of radius `thermal_object_radius_m`. This is linear area mixing, so a higher camera dilutes the signal and never amplifies it.
- `ε` is drawn from `RandStream(environment.rng.thermal_seed)`.

**EM** is the `phenomenological_em_proxy`:
- `response = bg + Σ G_i/(1+(d_i/d0)^p) + interference + ε`, where `d_i = sqrt(dx²+dy²+(standoff+depth)²)`.
- Nonmetallic materials have G = 0. Unknown materials are rejected.
- Above `em_model_max_standoff_m` the reading is `MODEL_OUT_OF_DOMAIN`, with NaN rather than 0.
- `ε` is drawn from `RandStream(environment.rng.em_seed)`.
- No detection threshold is applied.

**Invalid rows** keep their `sample_id`, `pose_sample_id` and `t_s`. The active channel value is NaN, and the reason is one of `UNCONFIGURED_SENSOR`, `POSE_INVALID` (out of bounds, in an exclusion zone, non-finite, or negative clearance), `DROPPED_SAMPLE` or `MODEL_OUT_OF_DOMAIN`. The opposite channel is always NaN.

**Firewall:** truth is read only inside the forward loops. The output has exactly the 11 contract columns, and the S-05 and S-08 checks confirm that no object ID, label, material or coordinate leaks into it.

Known limitations:
- **SIMULATION ONLY.** Both channels are illustrative proxies under ASSUMED parameters. The values in `mg007FixtureSettings.m` do not come from any real camera, coil, soil or object, and the 293.15 K baseline is a numerical input. Nothing here establishes buried-object detectability, detection range or accuracy.
- Thermal: no PDE solution, moisture transport, emissivity, sun or shadow, wind or optics. The diurnal "cosine" mode is a simple phase assumption. Objects are discs with one shared radius `thermal_object_radius_m`.
- EM: the falloff `1/(1+(d/d0)^p)` is a shape assumption, not a field law. There is no orientation, frequency, soil conductivity or coil geometry model. `em_model_max_standoff_m = 2 m` is a declared modelling cutoff, not an empirical range. `em_footprint_radius_m` is reported only, as an output descriptor.
- Flat terrain (S0) only; other terrain types are rejected.
- Optional features that are off when their fields are absent: `em_interference_norm`, `dropout_probability`, `dropout_pose_sample_ids`.
- The `synthetic_cased_surrogate` EM gain of 0.2 is an assumption (the MG-004 material name does not prove metal content), as MG-006 requires.
- No thresholds or detections are produced here; those belong to MG-008/MG-009. GitHub CI does not run MATLAB, so all results above come from this local R2026b run.
