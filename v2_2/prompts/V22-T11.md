# V22-T11 — Draw any field on BITM, simulated drone survey of it, suspected-anomaly map (owner: Shabaz) · BUILD

**Goal (Shabaz's workflow):**
1. The operator draws an empty field anywhere in the BITM satellite view.
2. A simulated drone surveys that field.
3. The map shows **suspected** anomalies, honestly labelled.

**Basis:** research report recommendations 4–5:
- camera surveys flew about 10–12 m at about 2 m/s with 75–80 % overlap, and detection depends on pixels on the target;
- marks on real imagery must not be readable as real hazards.

## Rule change (decided by Shabaz 2026-11-21, see `docs/DECISION_LOG.md`)

Suspected-anomaly marks may appear on BITM imagery **only in this view**, and only:
- as **hollow** symbols labelled "SUSPECTED anomaly (SIMULATION)";
- with a persistent "SIMULATION — NOT A HAZARD MAP" watermark;
- with a coverage-quality layer.

**Synthetic targets** are shown only behind an "Evaluation: reveal SYNTHETIC targets" toggle. **No export** of coordinates as findings. Update `v2_2/config/bitm_site.json` with an `allowed_marker_labels` list for this view; keep `forbidden_on_imagery` for all other views.

## Inputs

- T4, T5, T9 modules (`mgv22.sat`, `mgv22.cutfill`, `mgv22.geo`), `bitm_site.json`.

## Steps (subagents: one for field-to-world/survey building, one for the map view and symbols, one for the run pipeline and tests)

1. **Field:** use the T9 draw tool on a satellite view.
   - Convert to ENU at the site anchor.
   - V2.1 needs a **convex** polygon: reject a non-convex one with a clear message, or offer its convex hull with an explicit confirm.
   - Limit the area (for example ≤ 2 ha) so the run stays short. Show the estimated run time.
2. **World and survey for that field:**
   - Scene extent = field bounding box plus margin, in ENU.
   - A V2.1 SurveySpec whose polygon is the field and whose home sits just outside it.
   - **Altitude from image detail:** compute the ground sample distance from the RGB camera model in `mgv2.sensors.defaultSensorConfig` and pick the lowest altitude ≥ 6 m (V2.1 limit) that keeps the full swath covered. Report the GSD.
   - Line spacing from the swath, with about 75 % side overlap.
3. **Synthetic targets:** RANDOM placement inside the field (count chosen by the operator, private truth); `lockScenario`.
4. **Run:** fly headless with sensors and the frozen detector. Keep the event list with scores, and record coverage per cell (how many valid samples looked at each cell).
5. **Map:**
   - hollow circle symbols at suspected-anomaly positions, sized by confidence;
   - a coverage-quality layer (grey = low, none = unsurveyed);
   - the watermark;
   - a legend saying SUSPECTED and SIMULATION;
   - an "Evaluation" toggle that reveals the synthetic targets and matched/missed colouring from `evaluateRun`;
   - a results panel with T10 statistics (detection rate with interval, false alarms per hectare, and "No detection here does not mean safe").
6. **No coordinate export:** if the user saves anything, it is labelled SIMULATION and contains no target truth.

## Tests: `v2_2/tests/testV22FieldSurvey.m`

- Field to SurveySpec: convex accepted; non-convex rejected or hulled with confirm; too large rejected.
- Altitude and GSD computed from the camera model and within limits.
- Public data only goes to the engine, sensors and detector (`mgv21.scenario.assertPublic`).
- A short run (small field, few ticks) produces events, coverage and evaluation.
- Symbols are hollow and labelled SUSPECTED/SIMULATION; the watermark is present; targets are hidden until the toggle.
- No forbidden wording.
- No image files committed.
