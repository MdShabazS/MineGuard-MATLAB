# V22-T9 — Reel-style cut/fill elevation map over BITM (owner: Shabaz) · BUILD

**Goal:** a cut/fill map in the style of the reference reel (CutSheetRX): satellite base, an elevation overlay coloured cut (red) and fill (blue), a drawn field outline, a best-fit design plane with slope and heading, volumes, contours and an elevation profile.

**Elevation comes from a SIMULATED drone LiDAR survey of the V2.1 virtual field placed at the BITM anchor.** It is not BITM's real ground. Every number and the overlay must say **SIMULATED TERRAIN (not BITM ground)**. Units: metres, m², m³ and hectares.

## Inputs

- T4 and T5: `v2_2/matlab/+mgv22` (`SatellitePreview`, `MissionOverlay`, `geo`), `v2_2/config/bitm_site.json`.
- Simulated LiDAR: `mgv2.sensors.SensorSystem(world, pub.mission, …)`. `LIDAR_TERRAIN` samples carry `points_enu_m` and `valid_ray_mask`. See `v2/matlab/+mgv2/+sensors/sampleLidar.m`, lines 40–62 only.
- V2.1 public mission and world: `mgv21.scenario.buildPublicMission`, `mgv21.scenario.buildWorld`.

## Steps (subagents: one for the maths and its tests, one for the UI, one for the LiDAR survey and gridding; you integrate)

1. **Survey to surface:** `+mgv22/+cutfill/surveySurface.m`.
   - Fly a V2.1 mission headless (engine plus `SensorSystem`).
   - Collect the valid `points_enu_m` from `LIDAR_TERRAIN` samples.
   - Grid them at 1 m (`scatteredInterpolant`, linear, no extrapolation outside the coverage: NaN).
   - Return `S` with `x`, `y`, `Z` (ENU metres), `coverage_mask`, `n_points`, `source = "SIMULATED_LIDAR"`.
   - Cache it to `tempdir` only, never in Git.
2. **Maths** (pure functions, core MATLAB): `+mgv22/+cutfill/`:
   - `clipToPolygon(S, polyENU)`;
   - `fitPlane(S, mask)`: least squares `z = a + b·x + c·y`; slope % = 100·hypot(b, c); heading = downhill direction, degrees from north;
   - `planeFromSlope(a, slopePct, headingDeg)`;
   - `volumes(S, mask, plane)`: per cell d = existing − design; cut = Σ max(d, 0)·cellArea; fill = Σ max(−d, 0)·cellArea; ratio; max cut and fill; area in m² and ha; cut per ha. NaN cells are excluded and counted.
3. **View:** `+mgv22/+cutfill/CutFillView.m`, a uifigure laid out like the reel.
   - **Left panel:**
     - field area;
     - cut and fill (m³);
     - ratio and max cut/fill;
     - **Best fit** button;
     - **Slope %** and **Heading °** fields;
     - **Contours** interval (0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1 m) with **Existing** and **Design** checkboxes;
     - **Cut/Fill** on/off and a **transparency** slider;
     - **Draw field area** and **Draw elevation profile** buttons.
   - **Right:** the T4 satellite geoaxes at BITM.
   - **Overlay:**
     - a cut/fill raster as a red–white–blue diverging colormap centred on 0, with transparency, drawn with `geoimage`, or a `geoplot` patch grid if `geoimage` is unavailable in base MATLAB;
     - existing and design contours (`contourc`, converted to WGS84);
     - a legend box "Cut … · Fill … m³ · SIMULATED TERRAIN".
   - **Draw tool:** click to add points, double-click to finish, **Undo** and **Cancel**, numbered vertex labels. Reject self-intersecting or tiny outlines with a message.
   - **Profile tool:** draw a line, then a small plot of existing vs design along it.
   - The banner and credits from T4 stay visible. The overlay never covers the credits. No mine, target or anomaly markers.
4. **Entry point:** `mgv22CutFillMap()` in `v2_2/matlab/` opens the view on a cached or new simulated survey.

## Tests: `v2_2/tests/testV22CutFill.m`

- `fitPlane` recovers a synthetic tilted plane exactly (1e-9), with slope and heading correct.
- Analytic volumes:
  - a flat surface 1 m above a flat design over 20×10 m → fill 0, cut 200 m³;
  - a pyramid (or cone) → its analytic volume within 2 %;
  - cut and fill of a symmetric bump/dip cancel.
- `clipToPolygon`: points inside and outside; a concave outline; NaN cells excluded and counted.
- Invalid outline (fewer than 3 points, self-intersecting, zero area) → clear error.
- `surveySurface` on a short flight → `n_points > 0`, grid inside the flown area, `source = "SIMULATED_LIDAR"`.
- View, hidden:
  - opens; the panel values match `volumes()`;
  - toggles and the slider change the overlay;
  - the labels include SIMULATED TERRAIN;
  - every drawn layer is labelled; nothing contains a forbidden word;
  - 10 open/close cycles leave no figures.
- No image files under `v2_2`.
- Timing: `volumes` plus redraw for the default field. Record the time.
