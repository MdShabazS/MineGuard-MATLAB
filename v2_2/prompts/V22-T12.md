# V22-T12 — Relief-anomaly layer: craters, trenches, disturbed ground and simulated flood change (owner: Shabaz) · BUILD

**Why:** research report recommendation 3 and notes `terrain_dem_role.md`:
- LiDAR terrain finds the war *around* mines: trenches, foxholes, craters (HALO Trust, Angola: foxholes about 0.6–0.8 m deep, craters about 5.9 m wide and 0.65 m deep).
- Floods and landslides move mines (Bosnia 2014).
- Cut/fill against a flat plane is not a hazard score. Relief against a **local trend** is legitimate **indirect evidence**.

## Inputs

- The T9 surface `mgv22.cutfill.surveySurface` and view `CutFillView`.
- The research notes named above.

## Steps (subagents: one for the maths and tests, one for the synthetic features and flood change, one for the view)

1. **Trend and anomaly:** `+mgv22/+relief/`:
   - `localTrend(S, radius_m)`: robust local trend, for example a moving-window median or local plane, ignoring NaN cells;
   - `reliefAnomaly(S, radius_m)`: surface minus trend;
   - `findFeatures(A, S, opts)`: connected regions deeper or higher than a threshold, with area, depth, shape and type guess (pit, crater, linear trench, berm). Default thresholds come from the published sizes, and are documented.
2. **Synthetic conflict features:** `injectFeatures(S, spec)` adds craters (bowl), foxholes (small pits) and a trench (long narrow cut) to the simulated surface, with the positions recorded **privately**. The layer must find them.
3. **Flood/landslide change:** `simulateSoilMovement(S, opts)` gives a before/after pair (erosion along a gully, deposition downslope). `surfaceChange(before, after)` gives the difference map with "where soil moved, mines could have moved" areas.
4. **View:** add a "Relief anomalies" layer and a "Soil movement" layer to the T9 view, or a mode switch. Label both **INDIRECT EVIDENCE – relief anomaly (SIMULATION)**, with a legend. Never call them mines or a hazard score.

## Tests: `v2_2/tests/testV22Relief.m`

- An injected crater of known size is found with depth within 10 % and its centre within 1 cell.
- An injected trench is classified as linear.
- A flat plane gives no features.
- Soil-movement areas match the simulated change.
- Labels say INDIRECT EVIDENCE and SIMULATION.
