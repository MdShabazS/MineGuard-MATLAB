# V22-T5 — Virtual V2.1 route over BITM, synced with the 3-D demo (owner: Danish) · BUILD

**Goal:** draw the V2.1 mission on the T4 satellite preview, labelled VIRTUAL and moving with the mission clock (ENH-011).

## Inputs

- The T4 module in `v2_2/matlab/+mgv22` and its tests.
- `v2_2/config/bitm_site.json`.
- V2.1 public mission data: `mgv21.scenario.buildPublicMission` returns `pub`, with `pub.mission.survey_polygon_enu_m`, `home_enu_m` and the planned route from `mgv2.mission.planSurvey(pub.mission)`.
- Poses: `MissionSession` engine `poseLog()` or `snapshot()`. Read `v2_1/docs/V21_CONTRACT.md` §5 for what is public.

## Steps (subagents: one writes the overlay class, one writes its tests)

1. **Overlay class:** `v2_2/matlab/+mgv22/+sat/MissionOverlay.m`:
   - `attach(preview, pub)` draws the boundary, home and planned route, converted ENU → WGS84 at the site anchor;
   - `update(pose)` moves the drone marker and its trail.
2. **Labels:** a legend saying "VIRTUAL survey boundary / planned route / simulated drone", and the site banner always visible.
3. **Public data only:** only `pub` and poses. **Never** priv, truth, targets, events or anomaly markers on the satellite view. Assert this with `mgv21.scenario.assertPublic` on the inputs.
4. **Sync:** the marker position equals the pose at the same tick. Add a helper `syncFromPoseLog(log, tick)` for replay or tests.

## Tests: `v2_2/tests/testV22MissionOverlay.m`

- The boundary and route vertices map within 1 cm on a round trip.
- No forbidden layer or field is present.
- The VIRTUAL labels and banner are present.
- The marker matches the pose at 5 ticks from a recorded pose log.
- `update` at 20 Hz for 200 ticks stays under a time budget. Record the measured value.

## Verify (Windows, R2026b, desktop)

- `runtests('v2_2/tests')` (T4 and T5 suites) and `checkcode` on `v2_2/matlab`.
- One live run: launch `mgv21MissionControl`, open the preview with the overlay, fly about 60 s, and observe the drone moving in step with the 3-D view. Describe it in the evidence; keep screenshots local.
