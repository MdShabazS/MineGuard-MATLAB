## Delivered

`v2_2/matlab/+mgv22/+sat/MissionOverlay.m` puts the V2.1 mission on the T4 BITM satellite preview.
- `MissionOverlay(preview, pub)`: draws the survey boundary, the planned route (from `mgv2.mission.buildRoute` and `planSurvey`) and the home pad from the **public** `pub` only, after `mgv21.scenario.assertPublic`. ENU (0,0) sits at the site anchor. Each layer is labelled VIRTUAL, with a legend; the site banner and credits stay visible outside the map.
- `update(pose)`: moves the simulated drone and its trail. The pose passes the same firewall. A tick that goes back (reset or new run) starts a new trail. It stops once the window is closed.
- `syncFromPoseLog(T, tick)`: shows the pose log as it was at a tick, for replay and tests.
- `bindEngine(eng)`: follows `MissionEngine` tick by tick. A second bind is refused. Steps after `delete` or after the window closes are safe.
- Never draws targets, truth, suspected events or any forbidden marker on the imagery.

Tests: `v2_2/tests/testV22MissionOverlay.m` (9). They cover:
- vertex round trip within 1 cm;
- VIRTUAL labels and no forbidden words on every layer of the axes;
- the firewall for V2 names and a V2.1 private name, for both `pub` and poses;
- marker equal to the engine snapshot;
- `syncFromPoseLog` at 5 ticks;
- `bindEngine` safety, reset and double bind, and closing the window while bound;
- update timing;
- cleanup on delete.
