## Delivered (`v2_2/matlab`, base MATLAB only)

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `+mgv22/+relief/localTrend.m` | **Robust local quadratic trend.** At each cell a least-squares quadratic surface is fitted over a (2h+1)² window (h = radius / cell). The normal equations come from `conv2` of the data mask and of the surface with the monomial kernels, so NaN cells drop out. After each fit, cells more than 0.15 m off the trend are excluded and the fit is repeated (3 iterations), so a crater or a bush does not drag the trend towards itself. If the local fit is impossible, a whole-surface plane is used. |
| `+mgv22/+relief/reliefAnomaly.m` | D = surface − trend. Default radius 5 m (a 10 m window, wider than a 5.9 m crater). Label: "INDIRECT EVIDENCE – relief anomaly (SIMULATION)". |
| `+mgv22/+relief/findFeatures.m` | Connected regions with D ≤ −0.3 m (pits) or D ≥ +0.3 m (raised), at least 0.25 m² (one 0.5 m cell). For each region: area, centroid, depth, and PCA length and width. **Types:** berm (raised); trench (length/width ≥ 4); large depression (width > 15 m); crater (width ≥ 3 m); otherwise "pit (foxhole-size)". Thresholds come from HALO Trust Angola sizes (foxholes 0.6–0.8 m deep; craters about 5.9 m wide and 0.65 m deep). |
| `+mgv22/+relief/injectFeatures.m`, `defaultFeatureSpec.m` | SYNTHETIC conflict features cut into the simulated surface: 2 craters, 3 foxholes, 1 trench. The answer key is returned privately and is never shown in a view. |
| `+mgv22/+relief/simulateSoilMovement.m`, `surfaceChange.m` | Simulated flood/landslide before/after (erosion along the gully, deposition downslope) and the change map with eroded and deposited areas. |
| `+mgv22/+cutfill/CutFillView.m`, `mgv22CutFillMap.m` | **Layer dropdown:** Cut/fill, "Relief vs local trend", Soil movement. Relief and soil layers are computed the first time they are opened. **Relief layer:** cells at least 0.1 m off the trend are tinted (purple = lower, brown = higher; the scale saturates at 0.6 m); hollow diamonds mark features; one label per type cluster ("3× pit (foxhole-size)"); berms are marked but not labelled. **Soil layer:** eroded cells red, deposited blue. `mgv22CutFillMap(DemoFeatures=true)` injects the demo features and uses a 0.5 m survey grid. |

## Self-review

Self-review: done by the owner (Shabaz's Claude), plus a fresh reviewer subagent (findings below).
1. **Trend and anomaly:** done.
   - The first version (moving average after removing a plane, 8 m) failed on the realistic surface. It produced 5 false "craters" of 52–952 m² from natural relief, merged one crater into a natural hollow, and missed the trench and every foxhole.
   - It was replaced by the robust local quadratic fit described above.
2. **Synthetic features found by the layer:** done on the realistic surface, with held-out positions as well.
3. **Soil movement and change map:** done.
4. **View layers with legend, never called mines or a hazard score:** done.
5. **Tests:** all the prompt's tests are present and pass.

Deviations, recorded honestly:
- **Grid size:** foxholes (1.2 m) and a 1 m trench cannot be resolved on the 1 m cut/fill grid. The relief demo uses a 0.5 m survey grid; the cut/fill default stays 1 m.
- **Depth accuracy:** crater depth is within 10 % (0.62 vs 0.65 m). Foxhole depths read 0.47–0.59 vs 0.70 m because a 1.2 m bowl is only 2–3 cells across. The test requires their location and type only.
- **Map wording:**
  - The site file forbids "anomaly" and "mine" on imagery (only the T11 view is exempt).
  - On the map, the layer is therefore "Relief vs local trend" and the legend says "Relief deviation from local trend (INDIRECT EVIDENCE, SIMULATION)", instead of the prompt's "relief anomaly".
  - The soil legend says "buried objects could have moved with it", instead of "mines could have moved".
  - The function and package names keep "anomaly", because they are not shown on imagery.
  - The T9 label test now also accepts "SIMULATION", the standard `mgv22.wording` term.
- **Tuning:** the defaults (radius 5 m, robust threshold 0.15 m, 3 iterations) were chosen on this one simulated field and checked on held-out positions. They say nothing about real ground, real LiDAR or vegetation.

Reviewer findings (fresh subagent), all fixed and tested:
1. **High:**
   - A 1.2 m foxhole centred exactly on a 0.5 m cell centre left only one cell deeper than 0.3 m. That was below the 0.5 m² minimum area, so the foxhole was dropped.
   - Every test position happened to sit on a cell corner, so the held-out test could not catch it.
   - Fix: the minimum area is now 0.25 m². Foxholes centred on cell centres were added to the unit test and to the held-out set. On the untouched field this adds 2 berms; it still has no pit, crater or trench.
2. The new view wording test used whole-word matching, which let "mines" or "anomalous" through. It now uses substring matching, like the older test.
3. The soil-area test only checked areas above 0. It now rebuilds the expected channel and fan on a uniform slope and compares the masks.
4. `setReliefRadius` cleared the layer before checking the radius against the grid. It now refuses a radius below two cells first.
5. If a layer failed to compute, the dropdown kept showing that layer. It now resets to the layer in use.
6. Comments: the foxhole width (1–1.5 m, not 2.5 m) and the PCA size bias (about 15 % long for straight shapes) are now documented.

Not changed (noted):
- `localTrend` falls back to a whole-surface plane where a local fit is impossible. This does not happen on the simulated survey (0.8 % NaN cells, none isolated).
- `surfaceChange` compares grid sizes only.
