Two subagents researched in parallel (web only, read-only):
- **A, licence.** Read the Esri website and services terms, the MathWorks geobasemap page, the MathWorks offline-basemaps page, the World Imagery item JSON and the service JSON. The Esri knowledge-base citation page returned HTTP 403 and was not read. Findings:

  - redistribution and derivative works are not granted, so no tiles or screenshots go in the public repository;

  - overlays are not forbidden, as long as notices are not obstructed.
- **B, location.** Verified the BITM address on the Ballari district list and the BITM contact page. Read the photo GPS stamps in BITM's own PDFs: main block 15.168147, 76.850379; counselling cell 15.168384, 76.850537 (about 30 m apart); sports gate 15.166225, 76.850736 (about 215 m south). Nominatim has no BITM entry. Recommended anchor 15.1682, 76.8504 and a window of 15.1653–15.1693 by 76.8489–76.8516.

- `geoaxes` + `geobasemap(g,'satellite')` + `geolimits` at the confirmed window → opened, basemap = satellite. MATLAB widened the longitude limits to 76.8477–76.8528 to fit the figure. The anchor is inside the window, which is about 290 m × 442 m.
