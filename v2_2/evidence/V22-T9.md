## Delivered (`v2_2/matlab`, base MATLAB only)

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `+mgv22/+cutfill/surveySurface.m` | Flies the V2.1 public mission headless (MissionEngine + SensorSystem). | `+mgv22/+cutfill/CutFillView.m` | The reel-style window. **Left panel:** area, cut (red), fill (blue), ratio, max depth, cut per ha, Best fit, Slope % and Heading ° fields, contour interval with Existing and Design checkboxes, Cut/Fill toggle and transparency slider, Draw field area and Draw elevation profile with Undo, Cancel and Finish, a profile plot and a status line. **Map:** the T4 satellite view zoomed to the field; the numbered outline; a red–white–blue cut/fill cell overlay (`geoscatter`, cells resize on zoom and pan); existing and design contours (`contourc`); a legend line in the map title. The banner and the T4 credits footer stay visible. |
| `mgv22CutFillMap.m` | Entry point: `mineguard_setup; addpath('v2_2/matlab'); v = mgv22CutFillMap();` |

Deviations from the prompt, deliberate:
- **Gridding:** per-cell averaging instead of `scatteredInterpolant`. It is faster and has no edge effects; there are about 104 returns per cell.
- **Coverage:** recorded as `count`, not a separate `coverage_mask` field.
- **Overlay:** `geoscatter` cells, because `geoimage` needs Mapping Toolbox.
- **Drawing:** map click callbacks instead of `drawpolygon`, which needs Image Processing Toolbox.
- **Surface type:** it is a surface model (DSM). The simulated rocks and bushes of the virtual field show as small cut spots. They are labelled SIMULATED and are not hazards.
