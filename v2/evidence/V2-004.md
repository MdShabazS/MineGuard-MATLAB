## 3. Key decisions justified by research

1. **Sensor authenticity:** RGB is scene-derived via geometry/ray projection; thermal produces **numerical apparent temperature in Kelvin** from declared surface/emissivity/ambient assumptions before any palette is applied; LiDAR produces geometric ranges/point cloud from scene meshes. Each is a separate modality with unique size/FOV/rate.
2. **Unified timing:** 20 Hz nominal engine, 5 Hz RGB/thermal, 2 Hz LiDAR, 10 Hz virtual navigation; these are **targets not measured outputs**. Every sample references exact authoritative pose/tick and unique RNG stream.
3. **Coordinate correctness:** ENU metres externally, tested NED at MATLAB UAV adapter, scalar-first quaternion with explicit transform and sensor optical convention. Camera center-down ray/ground intersection and LiDAR flat-ground range are numerical regression fixtures.
4. **Truth isolation:** private targets may be used by forward simulator and evaluator, never passed to public planner/detector/UI/export. Include nested-field injection tests and counterfactual label-change test with identical observations.
5. **Fidelity limitation:** synthetic RGB/thermal does not establish real-world mine detection; LiDAR cannot sense intact buried targets. Thermal broadband proxy explicitly not a calibrated LWIR camera, so environmental effects are used for realism and negative testing rather than claims.
6. **Scientific evaluation:** distinct new unseen grouped scene families and sealed **V2** held-out test, frozen before tuning; no V1 consumed TEST rerun. Report unit definitions, raw counts, false alarms, uncertainty and negative controls. No perfect fixture is called held-out performance.
