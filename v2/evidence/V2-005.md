## 1. What was built

A `mgv2.sensors` package produces four time-synchronised virtual sensor streams from **the same V2-002 flight** and **the same V2-003 world**, conforming to the frozen V2-004 contract **`mg-v2-sensors/1.0`**.

| Channel | Rate (period at dt = 0.05 s) | Output (public sample) | Model level |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RGB_NADIR` | 5 Hz (every 4 ticks) | 320×240 uint8 image, per-pixel valid mask, K, extrinsics, footprint/corner rays | Exact visible surface by perspective z-buffer of the shared triangles (terrain + rocks/bushes/markers, occlusion); albedo × Lambert (sun + ambient); Gaussian read/shot noise |
| `THERMAL_LWIR` | 5 Hz (every 4) | 160×120 **apparent** temperature [K] (NaN where invalid), mask, emissivity/transmittance assumptions, K legend limits, palette id | Private linearised surface energy balance per material + Stefan–Boltzmann broadband radiative proxy, PSF and NETD-like noise (V2-004 §2.5; not band-integrated, not calibrated) |
| `LIDAR_TERRAIN` | 2 Hz (every 10) | 23×180 organised rays (az 2°, el 45–89°, FRD), ranges, points in sensor and ENU frames, valid mask, per-ray reason codes, observed AGL | Exact first hit on shared terrain + objects; range = true + bias + N(0, σ²); per-ray dropout; 0.3 m blind zone; 100 m maximum |
| `VIRTUAL_GNSS_INS` | 10 Hz (every 2) | **REPORTED_VIRTUAL** position/velocity/attitude/heading, virtual lat/lon/alt, σ values, fix type | Reference pose + seeded static bias + order-independent drift + white noise + dropout; the reference pose is referenced by `pose_id`, never copied |

All samples carry the exact 19-field V2-004 envelope:
- **Identity and time:** `schema_version`, `run_id`, `scenario_id`, `sensor_id`, uint64 `sample_seq`/`pose_id`/`tick_id`, `t_s`.
- **Validity:** `simulated = true`, `valid`, enumerated `invalid_reason`.
- **Provenance:** `sensor_model_version`, `frame_id`, `coordinate_frame`, `footprint_enu_m`, `data_ref = "NOT_PERSISTED"`, `calibration_kind = "SYNTHETIC_ASSUMED"`.
- **Metadata:** `quality`, `units`.

The modality fields form an exact allowlist (`mgv2.sensors.contract`), and `validateSample` enforces it at the producer boundary.

## 3. Synchronisation and integrity (as implemented)

- **Single clock:** the V2-002 engine's integer tick. The `SensorSystem` samples tick 0 on attach, then only ticks the engine actually executed (via its subscriber). **A paused engine executes no tick, so no sample is emitted.**
- **Due rule:** a channel is due when `mod(tick, N) == 0`, with `N = round(1/(dt·rate))`, `N ≥ 1` and `|N·dt − 1/rate| ≤ 1e-9`. Incompatible rates raise `mgv2:InvalidSensorConfig` (v02).
- **Pose reference:** each sample's `pose_id`, `tick_id` and `t_s` are **copied** from that tick's immutable PoseSample; the sensor never mints them. Ticks must strictly increase.
- **Invalid data:**
  - thermal: NaN with mask false
  - LiDAR: NaN with a code per ray
  - RGB: per-pixel mask false (uint8 cannot hold NaN, so masked pixels are 0 and flagged)
  - frame-level `invalid_reason`: SENSOR_DROPOUT, NO_INTERSECTION, OUT_OF_RANGE, INVALID_POSE, or NUMERIC_ERROR (an explicit placeholder sample, never a fabricated value)
- **Truth firewall:**
  - world, mission and sensor configs are scanned recursively for forbidden names before construction;
  - every sample is scanned again, and objects (handles) are rejected;
  - the radiometric scene is a private property of `SensorSystem`;
  - the V2 world contains no targets at all.

## 8. Known limitations and unfinished requirements

1. **Mac: TESTED by Shabaz (§12).** 72/72 tests and a full ×1.0000 mission on Apple-silicon MATLAB R2026b. Only that one Mac configuration was tested; no other macOS or MATLAB version.
2. **1× depends on display refresh on the development host** (§11): ×0.999 at `SceneEvery` 4 and ×1.000 at 8 without screenshots; ×0.936 at `SceneEvery` 2; ×0.950 at 4 with 7 screenshots. All runs meet the provisional 0.8× presentation requirement.
3. **Model scope:**
   - RGB: no cast shadows, lens distortion (explicit zero), vignetting, exposure control or motion blur.
   - Thermal: steady-state energy balance with assumed material parameters and no thermal inertia history; it is not a band-integrated 8–14 µm camera model and not calibrated.
   - LiDAR: an idealised scanner layout (not a specific product), with no multi-echo or intensity.
   - Navigation: a statistical model, not a GNSS/INS mechanisation.
4. **Not implemented:**
   - no cast-shadow occlusion between terrain and sun in either camera;
   - no buried objects or hypothetical target perturbations (out of V2-005 scope, and the V2 world has no targets);
   - no run-package persistence (`data_ref = NOT_PERSISTED`), which is V2-008;
   - no detection, which is V2-006.
5. **Frame retention:** `SensorSystem` keeps the last `KeepFrames` full samples per channel (default 8). The complete per-sample metadata log, with checksums, covers every sample.
