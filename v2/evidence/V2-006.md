**Inputs.** The detector consumes only validated `mg-v2-sensors/1.0` samples, the public terrain and landmarks, and the mission `run_id` and `dt`. Truth fields are rejected by `mgv2.sensors.assertPublic` (d02). Changing only hidden no-signature targets leaves samples, tracks and events identical (d03, EVAL-05).

**Ingest policy.** Each sample is logged as `ACCEPTED`, `INVALID_SAMPLE`, `RUN_MISMATCH`, `TIMESTAMP_MISMATCH`, `STALE_OR_DUPLICATE` or `STALE_DATA` (d08, d09). Invalid frames create no candidates and no values are imputed.

**Features.**
- RGB: per-channel residual from a masked 31 px box mean, scaled by the frame's `1.4826 × MAD` spread; candidate where RMS z > 4.
- Thermal: `dT` = apparent temperature minus a masked 25 px box mean; candidate where `|dT|` > 1.0 K.
- Size: ground-equivalent diameter must be within 0.15–1.6 m.
- LiDAR: height above public terrain and roughness are terrain context only and are **not scored**.

**Score.** `support_m = (detecting frames / valid looks) × mean(min(strength / reference, 1))`. The fused score is the mean over modalities with at least one valid look. It is an uncalibrated heuristic, never a probability. An event also needs at least 2 observations and must not lie at a public calibration landmark.

## 3. Geometric mapping and uncertainty

**Mapping.** Each candidate's strength-weighted centroid becomes a ray through the sample's own intrinsics and `T_world_sensor`, then the first intersection with the public terrain (`georegisterRays`: 0.5 m march, then 24 bisection steps). A ray that misses gives no candidate and no invented location. Tracks use greedy nearest association (gate 1.0 m, one observation per track per frame) and merge within 0.6 m.

**Observation uncertainty.**
`sigma_obs = sqrt(gsd² (1/12 + 1/(4π)) + pose_sigma² + (0.1 m · tan(off-nadir))²)`, with an ASSUMED `pose_sigma` of 0.10 m. These terms are centroid quantisation, assumed pose error and assumed surface-height parallax.

**Track uncertainty (current code).**
`sigma_track = sqrt(1/Σw + weighted scatter + pose_sigma²)`, with `w = 1/sigma_obs²`, reported as `MODEL_PLUS_SCATTER`.

**Measured geometry (DEMO FIXTURE, clean clone `6263997`, same values as `1a02f03`; not an accuracy result).** Public landmark errors were 0.171 m and 0.098 m, against the AC-007 limit of 0.5 m. The fixture event was 0.081 m from its known position, with reported sigma 0.184 m.

## 8. Known model and uncertainty limitations

**Detector**
1. **RGB box-mean halo (known weakness, kept and now reproducible).** The 31 px box-mean residual puts a halo around high-contrast objects, which inflates the measured size. Objects well inside the 1.6 m limit are dropped. Output of d26 on a synthetic dark disc with the frozen configuration, fingerprint `fnv1a64:d6d364f988f04bb4` (measurement uses a diagnostic copy with a wider size gate; the frozen detector is unchanged):
   ```text
   r  4 px: true diameter 0.60 m | measured 0.59 m | frozen detector keeps 1
   r  5 px: true diameter 0.75 m | measured 0.77 m | frozen detector keeps 1
   r  6 px: true diameter 0.90 m | measured 2.03 m | frozen detector keeps 0
   r  8 px: true diameter 1.19 m | measured 2.58 m | frozen detector keeps 0
   r 12 px: true diameter 1.79 m | measured 3.31 m | frozen detector keeps 0
   ```
   This is one high-contrast disc on one uniform synthetic frame; the exact cut-off will vary with contrast and altitude. A 12 px disc is over the limit by true size as well. The effect on TRAIN and VAL has not been measured. It was not fixed because that would be tuning on consumed data.
2. **Heavy false alarms.** Target-free groups still produce many events (for example, TRAIN G04 has 58 fused FP with no added features). Causes have not been itemised per event. Scores do not separate false alarms from matches: a false alarm has the top score in the demo.
3. **No boundary filter.** Most VAL events lie outside the survey boundary (G07 fused 39 of 45, G08 fused 24 of 32). This is reported as is, because adding a filter now would be post-VAL tuning.
4. **LiDAR is not scored.** Protruding objects are labelled as context and are not suppressed.
5. **Blind spots by design.** No-signature targets cannot be observed. Visible target signatures deliberately overlap clutter, and all feature magnitudes are assumed rather than measured.

**Uncertainty and geometry**
6. **Not calibrated.** Sigma is a declared model. Its coverage was never measured empirically, and VAL has no matches to measure it against.
7. **Pose term is assumed.** Camera georegistration uses the sample's `T_world_sensor` from the engine's reference pose, not the noisy `REPORTED_VIRTUAL` navigation solution. The terrain is also the exact public model. Geometry results are therefore optimistic, and `pose_sigma` = 0.10 m is an assumption, not a measurement.
8. **Conservative double count.** `pose_sigma` appears inside `sigma_obs` (averaged) and again as the added term, so track sigma slightly over-counts it. d13 only checks the lower bound.
9. **Original CSVs are pre-fix** (§3).

**Evaluation**
10. **Small sample.** There are 2 VAL groups, 4 VAL targets and 1 mission per group. Greedy association may also split or join tracks.
11. **Synthetic only.** No result transfers to real sensors, real terrain or real targets. Coverage is evidence acquisition only.
12. **Platforms tested are limited** to one Windows host and one Mac host, each on MATLAB R2026b Update 1 (§13). This item originally read "Mac NOT TESTED", which was true before Shabaz's Mac runs. Procedure, kept for repeat runs (the suite is now 100 tests):
